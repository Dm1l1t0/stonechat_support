Child Safety Standards

Effective 26 November 2026 · The Italian version has the same content.

Stonechat has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). These standards apply to everyone who uses the Stonechat app, on Android and iOS.

What is prohibited

  • Creating, sharing, requesting or linking to child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form.
  • Grooming: contacting or befriending a minor in order to sexually abuse or exploit them.
  • Sextortion, sexualisation of minors, child trafficking and any other behaviour that sexually exploits or endangers children.
  • Using a profile name or picture that sexualises minors or promotes the abuse of children.

1. Adults only

Stonechat is only for people aged 18 or older. On first launch every user confirms their age and accepts the community rules. If you believe a minor is using the app, report the profile as described below.

2. Safety tools in the app

  • Report: available on every profile and conversation. The profile is blocked immediately and a pre-filled email with its name and ID opens, to send us the report.
  • Block: the conversation is deleted and that profile can no longer see or contact you.
  • Consent: nobody can message you unless you accept their chat request.
  • Text only: messages can contain only text. Files and images cannot be sent, and links are shown as plain text that cannot be opened from the app. The only image in the app is the profile picture, a small 64 × 64 pixel thumbnail chosen by each user.

3. How we handle reports

We review every report sent to jtengineeringsupport@gmail.com within 24 hours, giving priority to anything involving minors.

When we become aware of apparent CSAM or of a child at risk, we report it to the competent authorities, including the Italian Postal and Communications Police (commissariatodips.it) and the NCMEC CyberTipline (report.cybertip.org), we keep the information received with the report and we cooperate with law enforcement.

Stonechat does not use servers: conversations travel only between nearby phones over Bluetooth and we cannot read them or remotely delete content from users' devices. Blocking therefore takes effect immediately on the device of the person who reports, and reports are how we identify abuse and inform the authorities.

4. If a child is in danger

In case of immediate danger call the police right away (112 in the European Union). You can also report directly to the Postal Police at commissariatodips.it or to the NCMEC CyberTipline.

5. Compliance with the law

Stonechat complies with the child safety laws that apply to it, including Italian and European Union law, and with the Google Play and App Store policies on child safety.

6. Our role and its limits

Stonechat is software that runs only on users’ phones. The app has no internet access and we do not operate servers: we do not host, store, transmit, monitor or have access to profiles, pictures or messages, which travel directly between nearby devices over Bluetooth and remain on those devices. Messages can contain only text: the app does not allow sending files, images or openable links.

As a consequence:

  • Each user is solely responsible for the content they create, send or show through the app and for their conduct towards other people.
  • We cannot monitor conversations, verify users’ identity or age (which is declared by the user on first launch) or remove content from users’ devices. Our actions are limited to the tools built into the app and to handling the reports we receive, as described in section 3.
  • To the extent permitted by applicable law, we are not liable for content created or exchanged by users or for their conduct, inside or outside the app. Nothing in these standards excludes or limits liability that cannot be excluded or limited by law, or the rights that the law grants to consumers.

These limits do not change our commitment: we act on every report we receive and inform the competent authorities, as described above.

7. Child safety contact

The person responsible for child safety and for receiving notifications about CSAM is Jacopo Torroni, reachable at jtengineeringsupport@gmail.com.
