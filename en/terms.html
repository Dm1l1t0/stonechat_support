Terms of Use and Community Rules

Effective 23 November 2026 · The Italian version has the same content.

Stonechat connects people who are physically close to each other. To do this safely, we ask everyone to follow a few clear rules, accepted on the app’s first launch.

Community rules

  • Respect others: no insults, harassment, threats or discrimination.
  • No sexual, violent or illegal content and no spam.
  • Zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users: anyone who breaks the rules can be blocked and reported.
  • You can block and report anyone at any time.

1. Who can use Stonechat

Stonechat is only for people aged 18 or older. On first launch you confirm your age and accept these rules. The name and picture you choose must not break the rules or infringe other people’s rights, and you must not impersonate anyone.

2. Safety tools

  • Consent: nobody can message you unless you accept their chat request.
  • Pause and close: you can pause a conversation or close it, deleting its messages.
  • Block: the chat is deleted and that profile can no longer see or contact you until you unblock it.
  • Report: the profile is blocked immediately and a pre-filled email with its name and ID opens, for you to send us if you wish.
  • Verification code: compare it in person to be sure you are talking to the right person.
  • Text only: files, images and active links are not allowed in messages; invisible or abnormal characters are filtered out.

3. How we handle reports

We review every report received at jtengineeringsupport@gmail.com within 24 hours and reply to the sender when we need more information.

Stonechat does not use servers: we cannot read conversations or remotely remove content or profiles from users’ phones. That is why blocking takes effect immediately on your device and stays active until you revoke it. We use reports to identify recurring abuse, improve the app’s protections and cooperate with authorities when required by law.

In case of threats, immediate danger or crime, contact the police right away (the emergency number in the European Union is 112).

4. Liability

Each user is responsible for the content they send. The distance shown on the radar is an estimate based on the Bluetooth signal and must not be used to locate people. Be careful when meeting in person someone you met through the app.

Stonechat is provided as is. To the extent permitted by law, we do not guarantee that the service will always be available or free of errors. This does not affect the rights that the law grants to consumers.

5. License

The app downloaded from the App Store is licensed under Apple’s Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement (EULA); the app downloaded from Google Play under the Google Play terms. These terms and rules are in addition to those licenses.

6. Violations and changes

Anyone who breaks these rules can be blocked and reported by other users, and serious cases can be referred to the authorities. We may update these rules: the date at the top shows the latest version.

For questions, email jtengineeringsupport@gmail.com. How data is processed is described in the Privacy Policy.
