Stonechat Support

Stonechat shows nearby people who have the app open and lets you chat with them directly over Bluetooth, with no internet, no servers and no accounts. It is free on the App Store, in English and Italian.

How it works

  1. On first launch, pick a name and, if you like, a picture. You confirm that you are at least 18 and accept the community rules.
  2. With the app open and Bluetooth on, nearby people who use Stonechat appear on the radar with an approximate distance.
  3. Tap a profile and send a request. The chat starts only if the other person accepts.
  4. Messages are end-to-end encrypted and stay in the app’s memory. You can pause or close the chat, and block or report a profile at any time.

Frequently asked questions

The radar is empty

Both people need Stonechat open in the foreground, Bluetooth turned on and Bluetooth permission granted to the app. Bring the phones closer and wait a few seconds. If you blocked a profile, you won’t see it until you unblock it.

The app asks for Bluetooth permission

Bluetooth is used only to find people near you who have Stonechat and to exchange messages. If you denied the permission, turn it on in iOS Settings → Stonechat → Bluetooth. On Android the permission is called Nearby devices. Stonechat does not use GPS or your location.

The chat doesn’t start

Tap the profile on the radar and choose Request chat: the other person has to accept. If they just declined one of your requests, further requests are rejected automatically for about two minutes. If the problem persists, email us with your phone model, operating system version and the steps to reproduce it.

What the verification code is for

Every chat shows a code at the top. Compare it in person with the code on the other person’s phone: if they match, nobody is impersonating either of you.

Where my messages go

Messages travel directly from phone to phone and are never saved to disk or sent to a server. They stay in the app’s memory until you close the chat or quit the app. With Close and delete chat the messages are erased from your phone, and the other phone erases them when it receives the close request.

How to block or report a profile

From the profile sheet or the chat menu, choose Block user: the chat is deleted and that person can no longer see or message you until you unblock them in Settings → Blocked users.

With Report the profile is blocked immediately and a pre-filled email with its name and ID opens. Message content is not attached: describe what happened yourself and send the email only if you want to.

How to delete all my data

Settings → Reset identity and delete everything erases your profile, keys, block list and chats. Other people will see you as a new profile. Stonechat keeps no copies of your data on servers.

How to change the app language

Stonechat is available in English and Italian and follows your iPhone’s language. To choose another one, open iOS Settings → Apps → Stonechat → Language (on iOS 17: Settings → Stonechat → Language).

Does it work between iPhone and Android?

Yes. Stonechat for iPhone and for Android use the same protocol and can chat with each other, as long as both devices support Bluetooth Low Energy.

What to include when you write to us

  • Phone model and iOS or Android version.
  • Stonechat version (in the app: Settings → About → Version).
  • What you were doing and what happened, step by step.
  • For a report: the profile ID (format XXXX-XXXX-XXXX), and when and where it happened.

In case of threats, danger or crime, contact the local authorities immediately.
