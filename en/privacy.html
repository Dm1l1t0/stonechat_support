Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026 · Applies to the Stonechat apps for iOS and Android and to these web pages. The Italian version has the same content.

Stonechat is a proximity chat app: profile discovery and conversations happen directly between phones over Bluetooth Low Energy. There are no accounts, no chat servers and the app makes no internet connections.

In short

  • We do not collect or store your profile, your contacts or your messages on our systems.
  • Your profile (name, optional picture and ID) is visible only to nearby people who use Stonechat, while the app is open.
  • Messages are end-to-end encrypted, are never written to disk and do not pass through any server.
  • No advertising, tracking, usage analytics or third-party SDKs. We do not sell or share data.
  • We receive personal data only if you write to us, for example to ask for support or to send a report.

1. Controller and contact

The controller of the data described in this policy is Jacopo Torroni, developer of Stonechat. For any privacy question or request, email jtengineeringsupport@gmail.com.

2. Data that stays on your phone

To work, the app stores on your device:

  • the name you choose and your optional profile picture, reduced to 64 × 64 pixels;
  • your cryptographic identity (a key pair generated on the phone, from which your ID in the format XXXX-XXXX-XXXX is derived);
  • the list of profiles you have blocked;
  • the color palette you chose for the interface.

Profile, keys and block list are kept in the system’s protected storage (Keychain on iOS, Android Keystore-encrypted storage on Android), are not synced to iCloud and are excluded from backups. We have no access to this data.

3. Data visible to nearby people

Only while Stonechat is open in the foreground, your phone broadcasts over Bluetooth your name, profile picture and ID to nearby devices running Stonechat, so that you appear on their radar. When the app goes to the background you are no longer visible.

Your ID stays the same until you reset your identity, so someone who meets you several times can recognize your profile. The distance shown on the radar is estimated on the phone from Bluetooth signal strength, is approximate and is never transmitted to anyone. Stonechat does not use GPS or the device’s location.

4. Messages

A chat starts only if the other person accepts your request. Messages are text only, end-to-end encrypted (AES-256-GCM with ephemeral X25519 keys, connection authenticated with Ed25519) and travel directly from one phone to the other.

Messages are kept in the app’s memory only and are never saved to disk. With Close and delete chat they are erased immediately from your phone; the other phone erases them when it receives the close request. Quitting the app also discards them. Remember that the recipient can still read, copy or take a screenshot of a message.

5. Device permissions

PermissionUse
Bluetooth (iOS) · Nearby devices (Android)Finding nearby people who use Stonechat and exchanging messages. On Android, scanning is declared as not used to derive location.
Photos (system picker)Choosing your profile picture. The app receives only the picture you select, not access to your library.

The app does not request access to location, contacts, microphone, camera or the internet.

6. Support and reports

When you tap Report, the profile is blocked and your phone’s email app prepares a draft addressed to us with the reported profile’s name and ID. Chat history is not attached. The email is sent only if you decide to send it.

If you write to us, for support or to report someone, we process:

  • your email address and any name associated with it;
  • the content of your message and any attachments you choose to send;
  • for reports, the name and ID of the reported profile.

Purposes and legal basis. We use this data only to reply to you, handle reports and prevent abuse, based on our legitimate interest in providing support and keeping the community safe (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and, when you exercise your rights, to comply with legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

Retention. We keep correspondence for as long as needed to handle the request and no longer than 12 months after it is closed, unless it is needed for longer to establish or defend legal claims or by law.

Providers. Emails are received and stored through Google’s Gmail service, which may process data outside the European Economic Area under the safeguards required by law (such as standard contractual clauses or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework). We do not share this data with anyone else, except with authorities where required by law.

7. These web pages

These pages are static and hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). They use no cookies, forms, advertising or analytics. The hosting provider may log technical connection data, such as IP addresses, to serve the pages and keep them secure.

8. Children

Stonechat is intended for people aged 18 or older, as confirmed on first launch. We do not knowingly process data of minors. If you believe a minor has sent us personal data, write to us and we will delete it.

9. Deleting your data

Settings → Reset identity and delete everything erases your profile, keys, block list and chats from the device. Other people will see you as a new profile.

On iPhone the system may keep the protected storage after the app is removed: Stonechat erases it automatically on the first launch of a new installation. To delete everything immediately, reset before uninstalling the app. On Android, uninstalling removes all app data.

To delete the emails you sent us, write to us from the same address.

10. Your rights

For the data we process, you can request access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and portability at any time, and you can object to processing based on legitimate interest (Arts. 15–22 GDPR). We reply within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali) or with the supervisory authority of your country.

11. Changes

If the way the app works or the way data is processed changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app’s release notes.
